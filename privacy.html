privacy policy last updated: 03 may 2026

at camkit, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. this privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your data when you rent gear or book our event services.

1. information we collect

when you place a booking through camkit, we may collect:

  • your full name and contact information (phone number, email address)
  • government-issued ID details (collected at handover for verification only)
  • booking preferences (gear selection, dates, delivery method)
  • payment information (processed via UPI providers, not stored by us)
  • chat history if you contact us via WhatsApp or other messaging platforms

2. how we use your information

we use your information to:

  • process bookings, confirm availability, and coordinate handover
  • verify identity and ensure the security of our equipment
  • send booking confirmations, reminders, and service-related updates
  • respond to questions and provide customer support
  • comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes

3. data sharing

we do not sell your personal information. we may share data only with:

  • trusted partners helping us deliver gear or process payments
  • law enforcement when legally required

4. data retention

  • booking records are kept for the duration required to provide our services.
  • we retain records as required by indian tax and legal regulations.
  • id details collected at handover are not stored after the rental is closed.
  • you can request deletion of your records at any time by contacting us.

5. your rights

you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you.
  • correct any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

to exercise any of these rights, contact us at camkitr@gmail.com.

6. cookies & analytics

  • we use minimal cookies to remember your cart between visits.
  • we collect basic analytics to understand how our site is used.
  • we do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • you can clear cookies at any time from your browser settings.
